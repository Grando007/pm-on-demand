Questions

How does a fixed-price document work?

You tell me about your project, I confirm what's in scope, then I write the document with you and revise it once. Larger or unusual scopes get a quote first.

How are document sizes priced?

Small is under 5 pages, Medium is 5 to 20 pages, and Large is over 20 pages. Medium is twice the Small price. Large starts at three and a half times the Small price and is confirmed by quote, because very large documents vary the most.

What if I'm not happy with the work?

Tell me, and I'll refund what you paid for that document or those hours, and we both agree to end the work there. A refund is the full and only remedy. This guarantee covers documents and hourly time bought on their own; memberships (and the member pricing they unlock) are not refundable.

How is hourly time billed?

In half-hour steps at $95 an hour ($80.75 for members). You approve a block of hours in advance and I report the time used.

What does the membership include?

Two hours of my time, 15% off everything, the template library and priority booking, for one year. Unused included hours don't carry over.

Can you manage my whole project?

Yes. Under Ongoing management you can book single services (meetings with minutes, requirements sessions, sprints, monthly plan upkeep) or a monthly package where I run the project with you: meetings and minutes, timeline, status reports, risk alerts, sprints and steering updates.

Do you provide architecture or design?

No. I do not provide architecture or design of any kind (building, network, system or solution). Instead, you provide the architecture or design, and I draw it for you, or make my best attempt to, in Microsoft Visio: layouts, network and system diagrams, process flows and org charts. The content is yours (or your architect's or engineer's), and you stay responsible for it.

Is the estimate really free?

Yes. A short phone call to understand what you need, followed by a written price. No cost and no obligation.

Do you work in person?

No. All services are delivered online, by video call, phone, email and shared documents, so I can work with you anywhere in Canada and beyond.

What do I need to provide?

The source data and key information your project depends on (facts, figures, decisions, existing documents and contacts) must be given to me in writing, by email or shared files. I build the work on what you provide, so the work starts once it arrives.

What if my information isn't written down?

We can hold an online meeting where you and your people explain it. With everyone's permission the meeting is recorded and transcribed, and the transcript becomes the written source I work from. See "Data gathering by transcribed meeting" under Ongoing management.
