About

Project Management On Demand

Project Management On Demand (PM On Demand for short) sells professional project management documents and services a la carte. Instead of hiring a full-time project manager or a large consulting firm, you buy exactly the piece you need: one document, one meeting, one month of management, or a dedicated project manager.

Your work is done by a senior program and project manager with more than 20 years of experience delivering secure IT, infrastructure and cloud projects for government, defence, aviation and critical-infrastructure organizations: cloud migrations to Microsoft Azure and AWS, Microsoft 365 rollouts, data centres, secure facilities, and budgets of up to $33M.

Everything follows PMBOK and Agile practice, with the same discipline for a one-page charter as for a multi-year program.

Questions

How does a fixed-price document work?

You tell us about your project, we confirm what's in scope, then we write the document with you and revise it once. Larger or unusual scopes get a quote first.

How are document sizes priced?

Small is under 5 pages, Medium is 5 to 20 pages, and Large is over 20 pages. Medium is twice the Small price. Large starts at three and a half times the Small price and is confirmed by quote, because very large documents vary the most.

Can we work on time and materials, or a custom statement of work?

Yes. On time and materials, you pay for the hours and materials actually used, at the rates on this page, with regular reports so there are no surprises. For a larger or unusual project, we write a custom statement of work that sets out the scope, deliverables, schedule and costs tailored to exactly what you want, and you approve it before any work starts. Ask for either when you request your free estimate.

Do you offer facilitation?

Yes. A professional facilitator can run your meetings (agenda, neutral facilitation, minutes and actions the same day) or a half-day workshop such as a project kick-off, planning session, risk workshop, lessons learned or retrospective. All online.

How do you protect my information?

Everything travels over an encrypted connection, your information is used only for your work and never sold, shared or used to train AI models, and your files are deleted when you ask. We are used to sensitive government and commercial information, and if you have stricter security requirements, we agree a secure way of working with you first.

What if I'm not happy with the work?

Tell us, and we'll refund what you paid for that document or those hours, and we both agree to end the work there. A refund is the full and only remedy. This guarantee covers documents and hourly time bought on their own; memberships (and the member pricing they unlock) are not refundable.

How is hourly time billed?

In half-hour steps at $95 an hour ($80.75 for members). You approve a block of hours in advance and we report the time used.

What does the membership include?

Two hours of project management, 15% off everything, the template library and priority booking, for one year. Unused included hours don't carry over.

Can you manage my whole project?

Yes. Under Ongoing management you can book single services (meetings with minutes, requirements sessions, sprints, monthly plan upkeep) or a monthly package where we run the project with you: meetings and minutes, timeline, status reports, risk alerts, sprints and steering updates.

Can we hire a project manager directly?

Yes. You can hire a dedicated project manager by the month, part-time (about 40 hours a month), working online with your team for as long as you need. For larger projects that need more time, quotes are available on request.

Do you provide architecture or design?

No. We do not provide architecture or design of any kind (building, network, system or solution). Instead, you provide the architecture or design, and we draw it for you, or make our best attempt to, in Microsoft Visio: layouts, network and system diagrams, process flows and org charts. The content is yours (or your architect's or engineer's), and you stay responsible for it.

Is the estimate really free?

Yes. We review what you need online, by email or a short video call (or by phone if you prefer), then send you a written price. No cost and no obligation.

Do you work in person?

No. All services are delivered online, by video call, phone, email and shared documents, so we can work with you anywhere in Canada and beyond.

What do I need to provide?

The source data and key information your project depends on (facts, figures, decisions, existing documents and contacts) must be given to us in writing, by email or shared files. We build the work on what you provide, so the work starts once it arrives.

What if my information isn't written down?

We can hold an online meeting where you and your people explain it. With everyone's permission the meeting is recorded and transcribed, and the transcript becomes the written source we work from. See "Data gathering by transcribed meeting" under Ongoing management.
